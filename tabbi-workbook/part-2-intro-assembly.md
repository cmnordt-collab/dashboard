# CONNECTING / ASSEMBLY CONTENT

## Pick Your Profile

---

### SPREAD (LEFT PAGE): Which One Is You?

You've read the Elements. You know what Tabbi actually is and how it works. Now you need to know which version of it you're actually building.

Because the system is the same for everyone. But your life is not. Your constraints are different. Your anchor is different. What's on fire for you is different than what's on fire for someone else.

So you pick the life situation that's closest to yours. Not perfect. Closest. Because "closest" is where the chaos is specific enough to actually help.

Here are six profiles. Employed (9-5). Freelancer. Student. Parent. Caregiver. Job Seeker/In Transition.

One of them is probably you. Or close enough. Or a mix. That's fine. Read the one that resonates. Take what works. Leave the rest.

(And if none of them fit, you already have the Elements. You can build your own system from those. This is just scaffolding.)

---

### SPREAD (RIGHT PAGE): How to Use Part 2

Each profile has the same structure:

**Spreads 1-2:** Here's what your life looks like. The chaos specific to you. And reflection prompts so you can see yourself in it.

**Spreads 3-4:** A sample dashboard. What this system looks like when it's built for your life situation. And customization prompts so you can make it yours.

**Spread 5:** Your anchor. The thing that keeps you standing. How to protect it.

Read your profile. Answer the prompts. Don't answer perfectly. Answer honestly. Your dashboard is built on what's actually true for you, not what should be true.

Then keep it. Tear out the pages. Screenshot the prompts. Use them later when you're building.

---

### SPREAD (LEFT PAGE): If You're Between Profiles

Maybe you're employed but also freelancing on the side. Maybe you're a parent who's also a caregiver. Maybe you're a student and you're also working and you're also trying to apply to jobs.

Read both profiles. Take the parts that match your life. Your system is a mashup. That's not failure. That's you building something that actually works for you instead of pretending to fit into someone else's system.

Your anchor is still first. Everything else is built around it.

---

### SPREAD (RIGHT PAGE): If Your Life Changes

You'll probably switch profiles. Next year you'll have a different job. Or the kid will start school and suddenly parenting looks different. Or you'll leave the job and start freelancing. Or you'll be in transition and then you'll land and then you'll be employed again.

When that happens, you don't start over. You keep your Elements. You keep your anchor. You just rebuild the Part 2 stuff around your new life situation.

Tabbi grows with you. It's not a system you outgrow. It's a system you keep adjusting.
